# Story mode: sources

Every year, name, place and event the chapters state as history, with where it was checked. Chen Er, his brother,
wife and children, and his comrades (Lou Dayou, Jin Aman, Wang Laoqi, Ye Chunsheng, He Ergou, Zhu Yinglong, Ma Desheng,
Shen Xiaoyi) are invented; so are the river-flats massacre of chapter 1, the beach raid of chapter 2 and the fort
fight of chapter 6. They are kept inside what the record allows. Where the record disagrees or is silent, the text
says so or stays vague ("some said", "the histories do not make clear").

## The southern troops, the drill manual, the soldier's papers

- Qi Jiguang (1528-1588): recruited miners and farmers of Yiwu; dismissed 1583; died 17 January 1588 (Wanli 15/12) in
  poverty. [Wikipedia: Qi Jiguang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qi_Jiguang)
- The Jixiao Xinshu (纪效新书), the drill manual. Its law on falling back, the ear and the roll (卷三 临阵连坐军法):
  "凡临阵退缩，许甲长割兵耳，队长割甲长耳……回兵，查无耳者，斩"; "凡行列不齐，行走错乱，擅离队伍……俱治军法";
  "此临阵第一禁约": shields, spears and wolf-brushes carry no head-cutting knife and do not stop for heads.
  [Wikisource: 纪效新书 卷三](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E7%B4%80%E6%95%88%E6%96%B0%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7%E4%B8%89);
  the squad (鸳鸯阵, twelve men) [Wikipedia: Jixiao Xinshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jixiao_Xinshu)
- The waist tag (腰牌): name, age, county, height, face and beard, marks, strength in jin lifted, issue date (武备志 卷28).
  [Source Library: Wubei Zhi vol. 28](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/wubei-zhi-vol-28/page/6992cdcec8c0c1ab515dcb83)
- Liaodong's hereditary military households (军户) and colony land (屯田) taken by officers: the general late-Ming
  pattern, as the game's own `mingGarrison` description has it. Ma Desheng's family is invented.

## Chapter 1, 1587: the drought

- The Wanli drought-famine, 1585-88, mainly in the north; the famine of 1587-88 spread to the Yangtze basin by the end
  of 1587; rice prices up 1587-89. Not verified for Yiwu or Jinhua prefecture specifically: the text does not name a
  county or claim local figures. [Wikipedia: Wanli era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanli_era);
  [Climate of the Past 20, 2287 (2024)](https://cp.copernicus.org/articles/20/2287/2024/)
- Qi Jiguang's Taizhou campaign against the wokou, 1561 (Wang Laoqi's war). [Wikipedia: Qi Jiguang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qi_Jiguang)
- The gentleman's petition, the intendant's 剿, the massacre, the 137 heads and the three qian of silver: the user's
  story, invented as private events.

## Chapter 2, 1588: the sea

- Recorded wokou raids: 601 under Jiajing (1522-66), 19 under Longqing, 15 in all of Wanli (1573-1619).
  [Wikipedia: Wokou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wokou)
- Hideyoshi's ban on piracy (海賊停止令), 1588, issued about the same time as the sword hunt; pirates to serve a lord or
  disarm and farm; aimed at bringing sea transport under the regime ahead of the Korean invasion.
  [Wikipedia (ja): 海賊停止令](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B5%B7%E8%B3%8A%E5%81%9C%E6%AD%A2%E4%BB%A4)
- Raiders Japanese and Chinese both; heads shaved on the crown: the wokou's general make-up. The question of who shaved
  the fresh ones is left open. A higher price for Japanese heads (真倭) than for Chinese followers (从倭) could not be
  verified and is not stated.

## Chapter 3, 1593: Pyongyang

- 6-8 February 1593 (Wanli 21/1/6-8); Li Rusong's army ~43,000; Wu Weizhong with the advance, attacking the hill north
  of the city with the monks, wounded in the chest by a bullet; Luo Shangzhi clearing the wall; Li beheading a soldier
  who retreated and promising silver to the first man on the wall; Konishi Yukinaga escaping on the night of the 8th.
  [Wikipedia: Siege of Pyongyang (1593)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Pyongyang_(1593))
- The southern troops' attack on Moranbong's dugouts (牡丹峰土窟) and the Korean court's verdict that Wu Weizhong's
  merit was highest (宣祖实录; 李时发); the reward given as 300 taels in one account, 5,000 in another (disagreement
  kept in the text); Li's report putting the northern general Zhang Shijue first, northern troops above southern,
  merit by heads; the censors' charge that "half were Korean people" (半皆朝鲜之民), investigated, not proven.
  [孙卫国, 中国社会科学网](https://www.cssn.cn/lsx/lsx_zgs/202210/t20221024_5552438.shtml);
  [The Paper](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_16424242)
- Chen Er's beheading at the tent (the fatal choice) is invented; no execution for asking after the silver is recorded.

## Chapter 4, 1593: Byeokjegwan

- 27 February 1593 (Wanli 21/1/27); Li Rusong ahead with a few thousand horse; Kobayakawa Takakage, Tachibana
  Muneshige; Li's horse shot, Li Yousheng shielding him and dying, Li Rumei shooting down the attacker's horse; Li's
  report of 264 killed, 49 wounded, 276 horses; Japanese claims of 6,000-10,000 Ming dead; Li blaming Song Yingchang.
  [Wikipedia: Battle of Byeokjegwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Byeokjegwan)
- The peace talks, each side's envoys telling their court the other had submitted (Shen Weijing, Konishi Yukinaga).
  [Wikipedia: Imjin War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imjin_War)
- Not verified: whether any southern foot reached the field. The text says only that the foot came up the road behind.

## Chapter 5, 1595-98: Shimenzhai and Ulsan

- The Shimenzhai mutiny (石门寨兵变), Jizhou garrison, Jianchang camp, Wanli 23/10/2-9 (October 1595): Wu Weizhong's
  old southern troops back from Korea, fearing disbandment, demanding arrears (索饷), and resenting the unpaid
  Pyongyang reward; ringleader Hu Huaide (胡怀德), a 26-year-old ordinary soldier from Yiwu; ~151 killed in the
  fighting plus executions, "more than 160" in all by Sun Weiguo's count, against older figures of several hundred,
  1,300 or 3,300; called rebellion; Wu absent; the southern troops recruited again in 1597.
  [孙卫国, 万历乙未蓟镇石门寨兵变新考, 学术研究 2026(6), via 爱思想](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/178712.html);
  Hu Huaide put to the slow death (凌迟) and the 3,300 figure: popular accounts citing 明实录 and 朝鲜实录
  [The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_7436759). Jin Aman's execution is invented.
- Ulsan, Wanli 25/12 to 26/1 (late January to 19 February 1598): Yang Hao and Ma Gui, 40,000 Ming and 10,000 Koreans
  (Gwon Yul); Katō Kiyomasa in the half-built castle at Dosan; the guns unable to reach the inner fortress.
  [Wikipedia: Siege of Ulsan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Ulsan)
- From Yang Hao's biography (明史 卷259): two palisades broken, Yang recalling the troops because Li Rumei had not come
  and "不欲寅功出其上" (he did not want Chen Yin's merit above Li's); "风雪裂肤"; "镐大惧，狼狈先奔，诸军继之";
  副将吴惟忠、游击茅国器断后; deaths "殆二万", reported as "百余人"; "诡以捷闻" with Xing Jie; Ding Yingtai's "当罪者二十八、
  可羞者十"; Yang dismissed. [Wikisource: 明史 卷259](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%8E%E5%8F%B2/%E5%8D%B7259)

## Chapter 6, 1599-1618: Liaodong

- Gao Huai (高淮), eunuch of the imperial kitchens (尚膳监监丞), mining and tax commissioner in Liaodong; "扣除军士月粮";
  Wanli 36/4 (1608) "前屯卫军甲而噪，誓食淮肉"; 6th month Jinzhou and Songshan mutiny; Gao flees inside and accuses
  officials of "逐杀钦使"; recalled, his duties given to the Tongwan tax eunuch Zhang Ye. The tax eunuchs sent from Wanli
  24 (1596) to pay for the palace halls burned in 1596-97 ("营建乏资……矿税由此大兴").
  [Wikisource: 明史 卷305](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%8E%E5%8F%B2/%E5%8D%B7305)
- Li Chengliang (1526-1615): reappointed Liaodong commander in Wanli 29 (1601) at 76; Wanli 34 (1606) abandoning the
  Kuandian country with Jian Da and Zhao Ji, "尽徙居民于内地", 64,000-odd households, "以大军驱迫之，死者狼籍", "反以
  招复逃人功，增秩受赏"; "掩败为功，杀良民冒级". [Wikisource: 明史 卷238](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%8E%E5%8F%B2/%E5%8D%B7238)
- Hideyoshi's death and the Japanese withdrawal, 1598. [Wikipedia: Imjin War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imjin_War)
- Fushun taken in the 4th month of 1618 after the Seven Grievances; Li Yongfang's surrender, married to a granddaughter
  of Nurhaci (Abatai's daughter); Qinghe lost in the summer of 1618, its garrison of 6,400 wiped out (the 7th lunar month in
  Chinese accounts; the month not checked against a primary source).
  [Wikipedia: Battle of Fushun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Fushun);
  [Wikipedia: Li Yongfang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Yongfang);
  [Wikipedia: Battle of Qinghe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Qinghe)
- Liaodong's arrears and the emperor's Inner Treasury: the general record of Wanli's last years (the game's Bayara
  description says the same); no figure is given in the text.
- Not stated: the price of a company officer's post, the ages on the rolls. Those are Chen Er's camp, invented.

## Chapter 7, 1619: Sarhu

- Yang Hao as Military Superintendent; the oath (明史: 2/11, out on 2/21; zh.wikipedia: out on 2/25) and the beheading
  of the Fushun deserter Bai Yunlong as a sacrifice to the flag; four roads, "470,000" claimed, to meet on 3/2; heavy
  snow and the date leaked; Du Song and Ma Lin destroyed; Yang's recall reaching only Li Rubai; Liu Ting three hundred li
  in, killed; "文武将吏前后死者三百一十余人，军士四万五千八百余人"; Yang jailed and executed in 1629.
  [Wikisource: 明史 卷259](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%8E%E5%8F%B2/%E5%8D%B7259);
  [Wikipedia (zh): 萨尔浒之战](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%90%A8%E5%B0%94%E6%B5%92%E4%B9%8B%E6%88%98)
- Liu Ting's road: the grudge with Yang from Korea; out of Kuandian via Liangmadian; ten thousand-odd southern men
  (Sichuan, Huguang, Zhejiang, Fujian) and Gang Hong-rip's 13,000 Koreans; 30,000 Sichuan troops asked, 5,000 granted;
  Abdali ridge (阿布达里冈), Hong Taiji from the hilltop, Liu killed (3/4-5); Qiao Yiqi's suicide; Kang Yingqian
  "仅以身免". [Wikipedia (zh): 萨尔浒之战](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%90%A8%E5%B0%94%E6%B5%92%E4%B9%8B%E6%88%98)
- The false messenger from "Du Song"; about 3,000 Zhejiang troops killed in Liu's column; Gang's surrender; Ming
  strength 90,000-120,000. [Wikipedia: Battle of Sarhū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Sarh%C5%AB)
- Liu Ting's "120-jin" blade: told as the storytellers' figure, not as fact.
- The muster's forty empty names and Chen Er's beheading at the pole (the fatal choice) are invented; empty names on
  the rolls (空额) drawing pay are the period's commonplace.

## Chapter 8, 1621: the Hun River

- Shenyang attacked on Tianqi 1/3/12, opened from inside next day, He Shixian and You Shigong killed; Chen Ce and Tong
  Zhongkui's relief killed; Liaoyang reached on the 19th, taken two days later, Yuan Yingtai hanging himself "佩剑印".
  [Wikisource: 明史 卷259](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%8E%E5%8F%B2/%E5%8D%B7259)
- The Hun River: "我辈不能救沈，在此三年何为"; Zhou Dunji and Qin Bangping's Sichuan men north of the bridge, beating back the
  white and yellow banners, broken by hunger and cannon; Qi Jin and Zhang Mingshi's Zhejiang camp south of the bridge,
  ~3,000 men and ~2,000 household troops, carts, guns, a ditch, sorghum-stalk fence plastered with mud; Zhu Wanliang and
  Jiang Bi's relief at Baitapu meeting 200 Bayara and driven back; powder spent, then spears and sabres, armour padded
  with thick cotton; Chen Ce killed; Tong Zhongkui stopped from fleeing by Qi Jin, both killed; "独此战，以万余人当虏数万，
  杀数千人". [Wikipedia (zh): 浑河之战 (1621年)](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B8%BE%E6%B2%B3%E4%B9%8B%E6%88%B0_(1621%E5%B9%B4))
- Tong Zhongkui dying with Qi Jin, Zhang Mingshi (张名世; zh.wikipedia writes 张明世), Yuan Jianlong and Deng Qilong
  (明史 童仲揆传, as quoted); Qi Jin as Qi Jiguang's nephew: popular accounts
  [Secret China](https://www.secretchina.com/news/gb/2020/01/24/920456.html), not checked against a primary source.
- Qi Jin's title 副将 and the year his men reached Liaodong: the text says only "after Sarhu".

## Could not verify (kept vague or left out)

- The 1587-88 famine in Yiwu or Jinhua specifically.
- Whether southern foot fought at Byeokjegwan.
- The Pyongyang reward's true figure (300 or 5,000 taels); whether the censors' charge about Korean heads was true.
- The Shimenzhai death toll (160-odd to 3,300) and Hu Huaide's sentence, beyond popular accounts.
- Any 1621 order for Han men in Liaodong to shave their heads (the text does not say what became of Chen Er's family).
- The relative reward for Japanese and Chinese pirates' heads.
- Qi Jin's exact kinship to Qi Jiguang.
