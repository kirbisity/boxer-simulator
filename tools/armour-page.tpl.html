How armour protects护甲如何防护

Armour guards only the places it covers, in the metal it is made of. Every number on this page comes from the game.护甲只护住它覆盖到的地方，护得多少取决于它用的金属。本页所有数字都取自游戏本身。

The rule: what you see is what guards规则：看得见的才挡得住

Each armour is drawn piece by piece: a breastplate from the hips to the collar, a skirt to its hem, sleeves to the elbow or the wrist, greaves, knee cops, gauntlets. The game reads those same pieces to decide what protects each place on the body. Where a piece is drawn, its figures apply. Where nothing is drawn, the body is bare, or covered only by what is worn underneath, such as a mail shirt under a coat.每一副甲都是一件一件画出来的：胸甲从胯部到领口，甲裙垂到下摆，袖甲到肘或到腕，还有护胫、护膝、铁手套。游戏就读这些画出来的甲片，来决定身上每一处由什么防护。画了甲片的地方，按甲片的数值算；没画的地方就是空的，只剩里面穿的东西，比如外套下面的锁子甲。

So a blow is judged by where it lands. A hit on the chest of a conquistador meets his breastplate; the same hit on his forearm meets only his doublet sleeve, because his mail stops at the elbow. The hand is the end of the forearm and the foot the end of the shin: bare unless the kit has gauntlets, the samurai's tekkō, or sabatons.所以一击算多少，要看它落在哪里。同样一枪，扎在征服者胸口是胸甲挡；扎在小臂上就只隔一层衣袖，因为他的锁子甲只到肘部。手是小臂的末端，脚是小腿的末端：除非有铁手套、武士的手甲（手背铁片）或铁靴，否则都是光的。

One hit, step by step一击是怎么算的

  1. Where it lands落点

    The blade or arrow strikes a body part (head, trunk, upper arm, forearm, thigh, shin) at some point along it, measured from the near joint. The last 15% of the forearm is the hand; the last 6% of the shin is the foot.刀刃或箭命中某个部位（头、躯干、上臂、小臂、大腿、小腿）上的某一点，从近端关节量起。小臂最末 15% 算手，小腿最末 6% 算脚。

  2. What is drawn there那里画了什么

    The game looks up which pieces cover that point. Armour pieces count: plate, mail, lamellae, scales, small laced plates, brigandine plates, iron splints. A cloth surcoat or a robe does not, unless the whole armour is quilted cloth, like a gambeson.游戏查出覆盖这一点的是哪些甲片。算数的是甲：板甲、锁子甲、札甲、鳞甲、小札、布面甲的甲片、铁条。布罩袍和长袍不算，除非整副甲本来就是棉布做的，比如棉甲。

  3. Which figures apply用哪组数值

    Under the armour's own material, the armour's figures: the share of a typical cut, point and blow it stops. Under a different material, that material's figures (mail sleeves under a breastplate count as mail). Under nothing, bare. A part the armour names on purpose, such as boots or quilted sleeves, keeps its figures.落在这副甲本身的材料上，就用这副甲的数值：一次普通的砍、刺、钝击，它能挡下几成。落在别的材料上，用那种材料的数值（胸甲下面的锁子袖按锁子甲算）。什么都没有，就是光的。甲特意写明的部位，比如靴子、棉袖，保留它自己的数值。

  4. The metal金属

    The figures are set for plain wrought iron. A harder metal takes more energy to cut or pierce, so its figures against edges and points rise; a softer one's fall. A blow is spread the same in any metal.数值按普通熟铁设定。金属越硬，砍穿、刺穿需要的能量越大，对刃和尖的防护就越高；越软越低。钝击被摊开的程度跟金属软硬无关。

  5. Gaps, then the result缝隙，然后出结果

    Rigid armour has openings (armpit, groin, throat, eye slit, the bend of a joint). A thrust may find one, and then meets only what is underneath. Otherwise the share the armour stops is taken off the blow, and an edge or a point must bring more energy than the armour's proof to go through at all.硬甲有缝隙（腋下、腹股沟、咽喉、眼缝、关节弯处）。刺击可能正好找到缝，那就只碰到里面的东西。否则就从这一击里扣掉甲挡下的那一份；刃或尖带的能量还得超过这副甲的“耐受能量”，才能穿得进去。

Metal quality金属品质

A plate's resistance to an edge or a point rises with its hardness. Alan Williams measured the plate he sectioned at about 100 VPH (Vickers hardness) for slag-laden bloomery iron, 150 for wrought iron, 200 to 250 for an air-cooled steel, and 350 to 450 for one quenched and tempered. The game scales the energy needed to cut or pierce by each metal's proof factor. The last two columns show one breastplate, set at cut 90% and point 85% in iron, made in each metal.甲板对刃和尖的抵抗随硬度上升。Alan Williams 切片测量过的甲板：夹渣的块炼铁约 100 VPH（维氏硬度），熟铁约 150，空冷的钢 200 到 250，淬火回火的钢 350 到 450。游戏按每种金属的“耐受倍数”放大砍穿、刺穿所需的能量。最后两列是同一块胸甲（熟铁时挡砍 90%、挡刺 85%）换成各种金属后的数值。

Armour you cannot see看不见的甲

Some armour is hidden or drawn by its own model, so each kit declares it: Guan Yu's gilt scale under his green robe, the rōnin's folding tatami-dō sewn inside his kosode, the ō-yoroi's box armour, the exoframe's plates. Clothes that guard a little, such as boots, breeches and the quilted sleeves under a Ming vest, are marked as worn and keep their small figures.有些甲藏在衣服里，或者由专门的模型画出来，所以每套装备都要自己声明：关羽绿袍下的金鳞甲、浪人缝在小袖里的折叠叠具足、大铠的箱形胴、外骨骼的甲板。能挡一点的衣物，比如靴子、马裤、明军背心下面的棉袖，标为“穿着”，保留它们不大的数值。

Why it is built this way为什么这样设计

Before, each armour had one number that spread over the whole man, so a bare hand or an uncovered shin counted as armoured. Tying protection to the drawing keeps the two in step for every armour, including ones added later, and a test checks that no armour figure applies where nothing is drawn.以前每副甲只有一个数值，铺满全身，所以光着的手、露着的小腿也算有甲。把防护和画出来的甲绑在一起，每副甲（包括以后新加的）都会保持一致；还有一条测试专门检查：没画甲的地方不许有甲的数值。
